    FAQ

    How do I order a custom knife?

    At this time, I am currently not taking any custom orders. My focus is to scale the availability of my knives to a point where weekly drops are made to my email list. If you are interested in a knife, signing up here is currently the best way to purchase one.

    How do I get one of your knives from instagram?

    I'm shifting from selling knives on Instagram to notifying my email list about their availability and processing sales through my website. Social media platforms currently restrict knife-related content more and more each week, so I'm moving away from relying on them for sales.

    Do I need a knife spa?

    Yes, if your knife has lost its edge, is showing significant surface wear and tear, or is experiencing performance issues unrelated to a manufacturer's defect, it's time to send it in for a spa.

    Why are your knives so expensive?

    My pricing is based on the high-quality materials and craftsmanship that go into making each knife. I use premium American-made stainless steel and titanium hardware, all contributing to an all-titanium construction. Quality is my top priority throughout the manufacturing process.
